    *   Interactive scenario simulations
    *   Customizable communication templates

Users input their financial situation, marital status, and specific life events (e.g., considering divorce, significant asset changes), and the tool provides a side-by-side comparison of different filing statuses, estimating tax implications and highlighting potential benefits or drawbacks for each scenario.
*   **Core Features:**
    *   Interactive questionnaire
    *   Scenario comparison tool
    *   Estimated tax impact calculator
    *   Personalized recommendation report
    *   Document checklist for chosen status
*   **Revenue Model:** One-time fee per tax year/scenario analysis: $49 for basic analysis, $99 for advanced analysis including asset/debt considerations.
*   **First User Step:** A user selects their current marital status and answers 'Are you considering marriage/divorce?' and 'Do you have significant assets/debts?' to begin the guided assessment.
*   **Target User:** Married couples, individuals considering marriage or divorce, and those with complex financial situations who are unsure about their optimal tax filing status.
*   **Confidence:** High

*   **Pain Point:** "YouTube commercials are torture for people with ADHD."
*   **Product Description:** A browser extension designed for neurodivergent individuals to minimize digital distractions and enhance focus. It goes beyond standard ad-blocking by offering customizable features like smart ad suppression (not just blocking, but also minimizing visual/auditory disruption), content summarization for long articles/videos, and a 'focus mode' that dims non-essential UI elements on popular sites like YouTube and Reddit.
*   **Core Features:**
    *   Smart ad & distraction suppression
    *   Content summarization (AI-powered)
    *   Customizable focus mode
    *   Site-specific distraction filters
    *   Integrated Pomodoro timer
*   **Revenue Model:** Freemium: Basic ad blocking and focus mode free; Premium ($4.99/month or $49.99/year) for AI summarization, advanced filters, and custom site rules.
*   **First User Step:** The user installs the extension, clicks its icon, and is prompted to select their primary digital distraction (e.g., 'YouTube recommendations,' 'Social media feeds') to activate initial settings.
*   **Target User:** Individuals with ADHD, autism, or other neurodivergent conditions who struggle with digital overstimulation and maintaining focus online.
*   **Confidence:** Medium

*   **Core Features:**
    *   Automated account search (by employer/SSN)
    *   Historical account activity tracking
    *   Consolidation guidance
    *   Secure document storage
    *   Alerts for account changes
*   **Revenue Model:** Freemium: Basic account search and tracking free; Premium ($9.99/month) for advanced search, advisor access, and consolidation support; optional transaction fee (e.g., 0.5%) for successful fund recovery/rollover assistance.

*   **Target User:** Individuals who have changed jobs multiple times, those nearing retirement, or anyone who has lost track of past retirement savings.
*   **Confidence:** Medium
