### 5. AI-Free Game Seal

*   **Product Description:** A certification or labeling system that verifies games are developed without the use of AI in core creative processes, providing transparency for consumers and a marketing advantage for developers.
*   **Target User:** Gamers who prefer human-made content and indie game developers.
