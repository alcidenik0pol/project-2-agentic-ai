### 1. 401k-Rollover-Finder
*   **Pain Point**: Users with multiple past employers lose track of old 401(k) accounts, leading to anxiety and significant financial loss when accounts are closed or converted.
*   **Solution Description**: A service that helps users locate old 401(k) accounts. The user provides their SSN and previous employer details. The service uses database lookups and direct outreach to locate the funds and presents a dashboard of all found accounts with a one-click process to initiate a rollover.
*   **Target User**: Mid-career professionals (ages 30-50) who have had 3+ jobs and have likely forgotten or lost track of at least one old 401(k) account.
*   **Confidence**: High
*   **Core Features**:
    *   Guided old employer information intake.
    *   Automated search across public/private retirement databases.
    *   Dashboard of all found accounts with balances.
    *   Guided rollover initiation process and status tracking.
*   **Revenue Model**: Transactional Fee: Account finding is free. A flat fee of $99 is charged for each successful rollover initiated through the platform.
*   **First User Step**: User creates an account and is immediately prompted to enter the name and employment dates of their first previous employer. The system begins searching in the background while the user can add more past jobs.
*   **Evidence**: This idea is supported by the "Concerns about" cluster, which included a post titled "logged into my old 401K, it was converted and reduced to 0; i don't know where the money went" (2146 upvotes).

### 2. ShiftGuard
*   **Pain Point**: Managers ignore employees' pre-stated availability and disrespect their personal time by scheduling them for last-minute or conflicting shifts.
*   **Solution Description**: A mobile and web app for hourly employees to lock in their availability. If a manager schedules them during a blocked time, the system automatically sends a professional notification flagging the conflict, creating a digital paper trail.
*   **Target User**: Hourly or shift-based workers in retail, food service, or healthcare.
*   **Confidence**: High
*   **Core Features**:
    *   Availability calendar with recurring blackout dates.
    *   Automated conflict detection and alerts.
    *   Shareable availability link for managers.
    *   Templated conflict notifications and a shift swap request board.
*   **Revenue Model**: Freemium: Free for individual employees. A Team Plan for $5/mo per user (paid by the business) provides a manager dashboard and scheduling integration.
*   **First User Step**: User signs up, connects their calendar, and is prompted to block out their first recurring unavailability (e.g., for a weekly class).
*   **Evidence**: This is based on the "boss ignored availability" cluster, which had an extremely high engagement of 8085 upvotes across only 2 posts.

### 3. FocusFlow for YouTube
*   **Pain Point**: For people with ADHD, focus-breaking commercials and distracting UI elements on platforms like YouTube are "torture" and derail productivity.
*   **Solution Description**: A browser extension for YouTube that not only blocks ads but also allows users to toggle the visibility of the recommended videos sidebar, comments, and homepage feed. A "Focus Session" timer hides these elements for a set duration.
*   **Target User**: Students and professionals, particularly those with ADHD, who use YouTube for learning or work.
*   **Confidence**: Medium
*   **Core Features**:
    *   Complete YouTube ad blocking.
    *   One-click toggles for the sidebar, comments, and feed.
    *   Timed "Focus Sessions" and a customizable "calm" interface.
*   **Revenue Model**: Subscription: $4/month or $39/year after a 7-day free trial.
*   **First User Step**: User installs the extension. On their first visit to YouTube, a pop-up asks, "Want to start a 25-minute focus session?"
*   **Evidence**: This idea comes from the "constant mental clutter" cluster, supported by a post titled "YouTube commercials are torture for people with ADHD." (748 upvotes).

### 4. Lexicon Scripts
*   **Pain Point**: People with executive dysfunction struggle to explain their challenges at work without "sounding like youre making excuses for being lazy."
*   **Solution Description**: A web app that generates professionally worded scripts for difficult workplace conversations. A user selects a scenario (e.g., "Explaining a missed deadline"), and the tool generates script options for email, Slack, or verbal delivery with an adjustable tone.
*   **Target User**: Neurodivergent professionals, junior employees, and non-confrontational individuals who experience anxiety around workplace communication.
*   **Confidence**: Medium
*   **Core Features**:
    *   Library of common workplace scenarios.
    *   AI-powered script generation with tone adjustment.
    *   Output formats for email, Slack, and verbal delivery.
*   **Revenue Model**: Freemium: 5 free script generations per month. A Pro Plan for $7/month offers unlimited generations and customization.
*   **First User Step**: After signing up, the user clicks a category like "Boundaries," then "Declining a last-minute request," and immediately sees three generated script options.
*   **Evidence**: This is from the "constant mental clutter" cluster, based on a post asking, "How do you explain executive dysfunction without sounding like youre making excuses for being lazy?" (673 upvotes).

### 5. FairFile Tax
*   **Pain Point**: Users of tax software are frustrated by opaque pricing and unexpected, mandatory upgrade costs encountered midway through filing.
*   **Solution Description**: A web-based tax filing software with a "No Surprise Upgrades" guarantee. The system provides a firm, upfront price based on a simple questionnaire before the user starts filing.
*   **Target User**: Price-sensitive DIY tax filers who are frustrated by the predatory pricing models of market leaders.
*   **Confidence**: Low
*   **Core Features**:
    *   Upfront price calculator based on tax situation.
    *   Guaranteed price lock-in.
    *   Minimalist data-entry UI and direct import from payroll providers.
*   **Revenue Model**: Tiered Flat-Fee: Prices range from $19 to $89 based on complexity, with the price calculated and guaranteed upfront.
*   **First User Step**: The landing page features a "Calculate Your Price" button. The user answers 5-7 questions and receives a guaranteed price before creating an account.
*   **Evidence**: This idea is supported by the "Unexpected upgrade costs" cluster. The low confidence is due to the high technical and legal barrier to entry in the tax software market.
