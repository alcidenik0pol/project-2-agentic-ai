Core principles


Core truth:
Other people's frustration is your opportunity. Painpan doesn't soften that. It owns it.

Tone:
Dry. Direct. Slightly dark humor. Never corporate, never hustle-bro. The brand knows what it's doing (mining suffering for profit) and finds it quietly funny rather than apologetic about it.
Think a geologist, not a startup founder. Calm, methodical, a little deadpan (the word is in the name).

Voice rules:

Short sentences. No fluff.
Verbs over adjectives. "Pan it." not "Discover amazing opportunities."
Metaphor stays consistent: mining, prospecting, sediment, gold, digging, veins, strikes. Never mix with tech jargon (insights, synergies, AI-powered).
Acknowledge the uncomfortable thing directly. You're profiting from complaints. That's the product. Lean in.

What Painpan is not:
Not cheerful. Not a growth hacker tool. Not "AI-powered insights platform." Not trying to be loved.

One sentence if someone asks what it is:
"We read what people complain about so you don't have to."
