*   **Pain Point:** Users struggle to find practical, relevant ways to integrate AI into their daily work beyond marketing hype, and lack clear guidance on how to use AI day-to-day.
*   **Product Description:** A web application that acts as a personalized AI adoption guide. Users input their job role and specific work tasks, and the system generates concrete, actionable AI tool recommendations with step-by-step instructions, templates, and best practices. It cuts through marketing hype by focusing on proven, practical applications.
*   **Target User:** Professionals across various industries (marketing, tech, customer service, etc.) who are aware of AI but unsure how to practically apply it to their daily workflows.
*   **Evidence:** The "Tech Disappointment and Avoidance" cluster, specifically themes like "lack ai guidance" and "marketing hype," and posts asking "How do you use AI in your day to day?".
*   **Confidence Level:** High
*   **Core Features:**
    *   Role-based AI tool recommendations
    *   Task-specific AI use-case generation
    *   Step-by-step implementation guides
    *   AI tool comparison and review
    *   Community-contributed use-case library
*   **Revenue Model:** Freemium: Basic access to common roles/tasks is free. Premium subscription ($19/month or $199/year) for advanced roles, custom task analysis, priority support, and access to exclusive templates.
*   **First User Step:** Upon signing up, the user is prompted to select their job role from a dropdown (e.g., 'Marketing Manager', 'Software Developer') and then enters a specific task they want to optimize (e.g., 'Drafting social media posts'). The system immediately displays relevant AI tools and a concrete use-case example.

    *   Code originality score
    *   Integration with Git platforms (GitHub, GitLab)

*   **Target User:** HR departments, business leaders, and operations managers in companies looking to integrate AI responsibly and manage workforce transitions effectively, minimizing employee fear and maximizing human-AI synergy.
*   **Evidence:** The "Workplace Management Problems" cluster, particularly the theme "ai job threat" and the post "Indian workers wear cameras to train AI on their jobs? Viral clips spark fear of AI takeover in factories."
*   **Confidence Level:** Medium
*   **Core Features:**
    *   Task analysis for AI suitability (augmentation vs. automation)
    *   AI tool recommendation engine
    *   Personalized employee upskilling pathways
    *   New job role definition templates
    *   Ethical AI implementation guidelines
    *   Impact assessment reports
*   **Revenue Model:** Tiered subscription for businesses based on employee count and features: Small Business ($299/month for up to 50 employees), Mid-Market ($999/month for up to 500 employees), Enterprise (custom pricing).
*   **First User Step:** A business administrator logs in and uploads a CSV of current job roles and their primary tasks. The system immediately begins processing, and within 30 seconds, displays an initial 'AI Impact Score' for the organization and highlights the top 3 roles most likely to be augmented or automated.
